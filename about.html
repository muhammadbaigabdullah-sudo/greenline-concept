About

Started with tools and determination.

Locally owned and operated, and still growing the way we always have: by word of mouth.

Our story

Customer service comes first. It always has.

Many of our customers remember us starting out more than 18 years ago, and we have come a long way since. We built relationships with our clients and grew slowly, one recommendation at a time.

We set out to be the total package, and that is what we have become. As full service landscape contractors we manage every part of a project inside the company: site preparation, underground utility work, hardscape, planting, lighting and the maintenance that follows. With everything under one roof, the work is finished without the gaps you get between several different contractors.

This business began so we could serve customers while doing something we enjoy. Standing back and seeing a project brought to life from a blueprint is still the best part of the job. We want our work to last for years, so we will not cut corners to meet a budget or a deadline.

Training

Technical education is how a better result gets delivered.

Over the years we took the formal steps to learn the irrigation and lighting trades and completed formal hardscape courses. Every employee is trained for the tasks they perform and certified where a certification exists.

Our hardscaping crew are all ICPI certified installers and take continuing education every year. We are an EP Henry authorized hardscaping contractor, and our lighting installers are certified through Vista.
